Bike Recovery

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Bike Recovery (“the App”) is a loan and vehicle recovery management tool used by recovery and finance agencies (each, an “Agency”) and their staff. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. It applies to everyone who uses the App: agency administrators, field officers (FOS), and repossession staff.

Who this policy covers

The App is business software (B2B), not a consumer app. Each Agency signs up separately, and an Agency's data is never visible to any other Agency. If you are a customer of one of these Agencies and have questions about your own loan or personal data, please contact that Agency directly rather than us — we act as their service provider, not as the party who collected your information in the first place.

Information we collect

Account information, provided when an Agency registers or adds a staff member:

  • Agency name, owner name, and phone number
  • Staff username, full name, and role (admin, field officer, or repossession officer)
  • Password (stored using one-way cryptographic hashing — we cannot read it back)

Loan and customer records, uploaded by the Agency for its own recovery operations:

  • Customer name, address, and phone number
  • Loan number, EMI, and repayment status
  • Vehicle registration number, chassis number, engine number, and make

This data is entered or uploaded by the Agency itself. We store and display it back to that Agency's own staff; we do not collect it directly from customers.

Device and usage information:

  • A push-notification token, so the App can alert staff about new allocations
  • Basic technical logs (timestamps, error reports) used to keep the service running

The App does not access your camera, photo library, contacts, or precise location.

How we use this information

  • To operate the App's core features — allocating cases, searching records, and sending in-app notifications
  • To authenticate staff logins and keep each Agency's data separate from every other Agency's
  • To send renewal and billing reminders to an Agency's admin
  • To diagnose and fix technical problems

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except the service providers strictly needed to run the App (for example, our cloud hosting and database provider, and our payment processor for subscription billing).

Data storage and security

Data is stored on encrypted cloud infrastructure and accessed over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access is restricted to authenticated staff of the Agency that owns the data. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.

Data retention

We retain Agency and customer records for as long as the Agency's account is active, so staff can continue their recovery work. An Agency admin can request deletion of their Agency's data by contacting us at th.jairaj@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for use by adult employees of registered Agencies and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to th.jairaj@gmail.com.
