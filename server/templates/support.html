Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password.
Passwords are set and reset by your Agency admin, not through the app. Contact your admin to have it reset.
I don't know my Agency Code.
Ask your Agency admin — it's shown on their Agency screen and needed to sign in alongside your username and password.
Search results look out of date.
The app caches records for offline use. Pull down to refresh, or check your internet connection — new uploads sync automatically once you're back online.
My subscription payment isn't reflecting.
Payments can take a few minutes to confirm. If it's been longer than that, email us with your Agency name and code and we'll check it.
I want my Agency's data deleted.
Email us from an address associated with your Agency admin account and we'll process the deletion request. See our Privacy Policy for details.
